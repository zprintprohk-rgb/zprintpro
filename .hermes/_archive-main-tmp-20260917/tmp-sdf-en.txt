<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-[#333333] mt-10 mb-4">What's the Difference Between Same-Day and Rush? 3 Core Structures</h2>

<h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-[#333333] mt-6 mb-3">3.1 Size Structure (Letter / Half-Letter / DL Tri-fold) — Distribution First</h3>
<p class="text-base text-[#444444] leading-relaxed mb-4">Letter (8.5″×11″) is the mass-distribution and real estate standard with room for detail; Half-Letter (5.5″×8.5″) saves paper and suits event tickets and retail promos; DL tri-fold (3.875″×8.875″) needs folding, which destabilizes a same-day schedule — plan it as a 24-hour order.</p>
