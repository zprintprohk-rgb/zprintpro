1. How to Choose the Right Binding for Textbooks?

Binding depends on page count and use case. Textbook printing standard specs: A4/B5, 80-100g wood-free paper, perfect bound or saddle stitch (products.ts specs).

Saddle Stitch: 8-64 page thin booklets for workbooks, drills and handouts. Lays flat, most affordable. From 50 copies. See the saddle stitch booklet printing guide.

Perfect Bound: 64+ page standard books for textbooks and training manuals. Spine title printing available. From 100 copies.

Hardcover: Premium books, yearbooks and library editions. Hardcover book printing from 100 copies.
