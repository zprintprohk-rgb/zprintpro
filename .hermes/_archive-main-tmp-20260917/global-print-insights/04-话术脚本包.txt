04 · 触达话术脚本包（海外版 v1.0 · EN）

## 
使用原则：先给价值、不强推、被拒即停；禁止绝对化表述（cheapest / best / guaranteed）。
语气：对小企业主用 casual-professional（友好、直接、不端着）。
时差提示：北京 15:00–23:00 覆盖欧美工作时段；首触尽量落在对方上午。
合规：每类渠道先看《06 合规》；私信仅针对公开发出需求的对象。
一、平台私信（Instagram / Facebook / TikTok）

## 
A. 回复"求推荐"帖

### 
Hi! Saw your post looking for a printing partner. I work with a print manufacturer that handles small batches — stickers, packaging boxes, booklets, labels, calendars and more. If you share what you need (product, quantity, size), I can send a quick reference quote for comparison. No pressure.
B. 回复问价帖

### 
Saw you&#x27;re pricing out {product}. Price mostly depends on quantity, size, material and finishing. Send me those details and I&#x27;ll get you a real quote you can compare — takes me about a day.
C. 软搭话（对方晒产品/开箱）

### 
Love what you did with {product}! If you ever need these produced in small batches, we do print manufacturing — happy to share options whenever helpful.
二、Reddit（先评论，后私信）

## 
评论（公开、给价值）：
- 
For small batches, look for a digital-printing manufacturer and ask each quote to include the full spec (size, material, finishing, quantity) — much easier to compare that way.
私信（仅当对方回复/表示需要时）：
- 
Following up from the thread — I work with a print manufacturer for small-batch production. Want me to put together a reference quote for your {product}?

七、报价引导

## 
先要件再报价：缺数量/规格时给"影响价格的因素 + 参考区间"，不硬报价；
- 给档位：economy / standard / premium 三档选项；
- 留后手："Final price confirmed after spec review and sample.";
- 不打价格战：被说贵时用"同规格比价"（见下）。
- 
八、异议处理（8 条）

## 
异议 | 应对话术 | 

"Too expensive" | "Understood — may I check the spec you compared? Often the difference is paper thickness, finishing, or quantity tier. I can also send a more economy option." | 

"I use a local printer" | "Makes sense! Keep us as a backup for peak seasons or when they&#x27;re backed up — we do fast samples." | 

"Is shipping included?" | "Depends on destination and weight — share the address and I&#x27;ll quote door-to-door options." | 

"How do I know the quality?" | "We can start with a sample or a small pilot order before a bigger run." | 

"What&#x27;s your MOQ?" | "For most products small batches are fine — it depends on the process. Tell me the item and target quantity." | 

"Lead time?" | "Typically a few days for samples; production timing depends on quantity and finishing — confirmed per order." | 

"Payment terms?" | "Standard is deposit + balance; options depend on order size and history. Happy to walk through it." | 

"Can I see samples/photos?" | "Sure — I&#x27;ll send reference photos now, and physical samples if you&#x27;re seriously comparing." | 


九、跟进节奏

## 
1. Day 0 首触（对方公开需求当天或次日）；


2. +5–7 天：带价值跟进（adjust 方案 / 参考价 / 案例图）；


3. +5–7 天：一句话提醒（"Shall I hold a production slot for your {product}?" 慎用，仅对高意向）；


4. 结束语："I&#x27;ll leave it with you — reach out anytime if it&#x27;s useful." → 状态置「搁置」。


十、合规提醒

## 
每类渠道遵守《06 合规》频次上限；同一对象最多 3 次；
- 被拒立即停止 + 记 Do-Not-Contact；
- 不使用 absolute claims；价格标注"reference quote, final subject to spec"；
- 邮件必含退订；私信在对方拒绝后不再发送。
