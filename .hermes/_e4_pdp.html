Interior Paper Selection+

Standard spec: FSC-certified uncoated 90gsm interior + 90gsm coated cover (full-color cover, mono interior). Text-heavy pages work well on 90gsm; upgrade to the premium option for full-bleed illustration work. Sizes: A5 (148×210mm), B5 (182×257mm), custom A4.

Bleed and Safe Zone (Read Before Uploading)+

Keep 3mm bleed on all full-bleed artwork; place key linework, dialogue and signatures inside the 5mm safe zone from trim. For saddle stitch, reserve a 5mm binding margin at the spine side of spreads so staples never cover key art. Convert all fonts to outlines and submit in CMYK PDF.

Turnaround+

Standard production is 5-7 business days, then DHL Express from our Shenzhen factory to Japan in 2-4 business days. A 24-hour rush is available before Comiket; during peak seasons (June-July and December) plan 10-15 business days ahead.

Use Cases & File Specs

Use Cases & File Specs

Doujinshi printing for Comiket cycles: ZprintPro prints your circle's book with a colour cover and monochrome text pages as the standard build — upgradeable to full colour throughout when the artwork deserves it. Orders start at 10 copies, so first-run circles can test reader response before committing to a reprint. Submit your file and a free digital proof confirms imposition and colour before anything goes to press.

Formats cover the two doujinshi standards — A5 (148x210mm) and B5 (182x257mm) — with A4 optional for art books and portfolio pieces. Text pages run on FSC-certified 90g dailin stock that reads clean in monochrome; covers use 90g coated stock that takes foil, spot UV or matte lamination when the cover art calls for it. Binding is a three-way choice: saddle stitch for slim zines, perfect binding for thicker issues, and folded formats for announcement pieces.

The Comiket rhythm is the deadline math: C109 runs December 29-31, 2026 at Tokyo Big Sight, which means finished books need to be in your hands before load-in — not printing that week. Ordering 3-4 weeks ahead of the event keeps proofing, production and DHL shipping (2-4 days to Japan) comfortably inside the window, and the same schedule applies to sale events and one-day circuits through the year. Our Comiket printing prep guide walks through the file checklist, and the doujin circle printing guide covers spec choices for first books. The full peripheral line-up — stickers, acrylic, posters — lives at doujin goods printing. Event dates and the load-in deadline countdown are on the Comiket C109 activity page.
