## §附录: 关键技术细节 (Appendix)

### A. PDP Hero 价格渲染流程

1. **zh-hk**: `convertToFromPrice(product.price_range, 'zh-hk', ...)` → `convertPriceRangeString` 命中 `if (locale === 'zh-hk') return priceRange;` → 直接返回 `product.price_range` 字符串 (e.g. "HK$1.5-30/個") → `convertToFromPrice` 取下限 (e.g. "HK$1.5/個")
2. **en**: `convertToFromPrice(product.price_range, 'en', ..., productSlug)` → `getIndependentPrice(productSlug, 'en')` 返回独立定价 (来自 `basePrice_en` 字段) → 渲染 "US$1.15/pc"
3. **ja**: 同 en, 用 `basePrice_ja` 字段 → 渲染 "¥150/個"
