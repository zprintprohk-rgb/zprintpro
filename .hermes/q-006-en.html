2. Three Main Box Structure Comparison

2.1 Lid-Base Box (Rigid)

Independent top + base structure, strongest ceremonial feel. Ideal for premium loose leaf tea, mooncake, and tea ceremony gift boxes. Stock typically 1200g grey board + 157g art paper laminated, can add foil stamping, embossing, and spot UV for premium positioning. Per-box cost US$ 1.00-3.00, 100-unit MOQ.

2.2 Magnetic Closure Gift Box (Reusable)

Magnet-adsorbed design, reusable 500+ times, perfect as "brand keepsake" for viral unboxing. Ideal for high-value tea and bubble tea VIP gift boxes. Per-box cost US$ 1.50-3.80, includes 2 rare-earth magnets.

2.3 Mailer Box (Moisture-Resistant Shipping Box)

3-ply B-flute Corrugated Fiberboard structure, crush resistance 80-120kg, ideal for cross-border tea e-commerce gift boxes. Compatible with DHL, FedEx, UPS standard sizes, water-resistant, moisture-proof, crush-proof. Per-box cost US$ 0.25-0.50, 100-unit MOQ.

5. Cross-Border E-commerce Five Key Points

  1. Size Standardization: Use DHL / FedEx / UPS compatible sizes (e.g. 350×250×100mm), avoid size surcharges (US$ 4 per 2cm oversize)
  2. Moisture Treatment: 3-ply B-flute + internal PE bag, tea is moisture-sensitive, critical for cross-border sea freight
  3. Structural Crush Resistance: Cross-border shipping must pass ISTA 3A drop test, mailer box 80-120kg crush resistance certified
  4. Eco Certification: FSC-certified stock + soy-based ink, EU/US customers' first choice
  5. Multilingual Print: English / Japanese / French multilingual print, avoid customs return for unilingual packaging
