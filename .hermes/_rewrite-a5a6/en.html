1. What Are the Exact A5 and A6 Dimensions?

A5 and A6 belong to the ISO 216 international paper standard. The A-series starts at A0 with exactly 1 square meter; each fold halves the area and adds one to the number. A4 folded once is A5, folded twice is A6. Every size keeps the same 1:√2 aspect ratio (about 1:1.414), so proportions survive folding — that is why one design file can scale cleanly from A5 down to A6.

2. Is A6 Half of A5?

Yes. Fold one A5 sheet along its long edge (the 210 mm side) and you get two 105 × 148 mm A6 sheets — the area halves from 310.8 cm² to 155.4 cm² (ISO 216 conversion). Read it backwards: two A6 sheets equal one A5, and four A6 sheets equal one A4. Memory rule: one size number up, half the area.

This half-relationship is practical: an A5 design scaled to 50% becomes the A6 version with no re-layout. One caution — body text shrinks by half too, so keep type at 7 pt or larger on A6 or it gets hard to read in print.

3. How Big Is A6 Compared to a Postcard?

A6 (105 × 148 mm) is close to a standard postcard: the US 4 × 6 in postcard measures 101.6 × 152.4 mm (USPS specification), slightly wider and longer than A6. A5 is roughly half a sheet of US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) — the classic size for countertop menus and price lists. A5 also fits half-letter rack slots, while A6 matches common postcard displays.

Artwork pixels at the 300 dpi print standard: A6 = 1240 × 1748 px, A5 = 1748 × 2480 px. Below 300 dpi, print will pixelate — check resolution before ordering; full file requirements are in the design file specs guide.

4. Which Size Fits Which Use Case?

The core trade-off is information density × distribution method — more information or sit-down reading calls for A5; mass handouts, mailbox drops and package inserts call for A6:

6. Why Tri-Fold A5 to DL for Direct Mail?

A5 tri-folded becomes 99 × 210 mm — the DL size. The matching DL envelope measures 110 × 220 mm (ISO 269 standard) and was designed precisely for tri-folded A4/A5 sheets. DL fits standard mail slots and US #10 envelopes, so the piece mails without an envelope — saving envelope stock and inserting labor — or drops neatly into one for a more formal look. For 4-page double-sided content, half-fold A5 or pick a folded leaflet format instead.

Direct mail ROI is measurable: Lob's 2025 State of Direct Mail reports 84% of marketers rank direct mail as their highest-ROI channel; USPS finds personalized direct mail lifts response rates by 135%. A tri-folded A5 gives you six panels — enough for brand story, product highlights, a promo code and a reply form — which is why it is the most-used direct mail format.

【A5 tri-fold = DL size (99 × 210 mm), mailable without an envelope; DL envelopes measure 110 × 220 mm (ISO 269)】
