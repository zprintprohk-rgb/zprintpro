CMYK vs RGB vs Pantone: 3-Color Mode Choices for Print

Print color = CMYK 4-color (Cyan/Magenta/Yellow/Key-black subtractive), screen color = RGB 3-color (Red/Green/Blue additive), brand color = Pantone spot (1 color = 1 formula). These three are NOT interchangeable — mixing them creates errors.

3 Color Modes Quick Reference

CMYK = print essential (4-color subtractive) / RGB = screen-only (3-color additive) / Pantone spot = 1-color 1-formula pre-mixed (brand color). AI/ID/PSD must convert to CMYK before print, else output 30-40% blue/purple shift. Pantone spot extra $0.04-0.10 USD/sheet.

2.1 CMYK 4-Color Proportion Quick Reference: C/M/Y/K 0-100%

Print CMYK 4-color = Cyan + Magenta + Yellow + Key-black, each 0-100% in 101 levels. Standard color CMYK formula quick reference:

Answer Nugget: [CMYK 4-Color Proportion Principles]

CMYK 3 principles: ① K (black) not exceed 70% (else dead black no depth) ② 4-color total not exceed 300% (else paper wetness) ③ Large black area use K=100 + C=60 (deep black) or K=100 (pure black). Pantone formula = C/M/Y/K fixed ratio, globally standardized.

Q1: What is the size difference between A5 and A6?
A: A5 is 148 × 210 mm; A6 is 105 × 148 mm. A5 has 2x the area of A6. A5 suits flyers, magazine inner pages and tri-fold brochures; A6 suits postcards, booklet inner pages and loyalty cards. Printing starts at 100 pieces at HK$0.50-2/sheet on CMYK double-sided 157gsm coated paper.

Q2: Must print files use CMYK, or can I send RGB?
A: Print files must use CMYK, because RGB cannot be printed directly. CMYK is the four-colour subtractive mix of cyan, magenta, yellow and black and is the printing standard; RGB is a three-colour additive screen space, so printing it directly shifts 30-40% off what you see, usually too blue or purple. Before exporting from AI, ID or PSD, switch to CMYK with 300 DPI and 3mm bleed.

Q3: When do I need Pantone spot colours and what do they cost?
A: Use Pantone spot colours when brand colour consistency matters. Pantone keeps one brand colour identical across batches and across materials, which is why chain brands rely on it. The surcharge is HK$0.30-0.80 per sheet in a single colour, depending on volume and the number of colours. Full-colour images and gradients are fine in CMYK four-colour, so no spot colour is needed.

Q4: Digital or offset printing — which saves money?
A: It depends on quantity. Choose digital for 100-300 pieces and offset above 500. Digital covers short runs of 100-500 pieces at HK$0.50-3 per sheet with no plate fee and 24-48 hour delivery. Offset covers long runs of 500-50,000+ at HK$0.20-1.50 per sheet plus a HK$300-800 plate fee and 5-7 day delivery. Above 500 pieces offset saves 30-50% per sheet.

Q5: What DPI and bleed do print files need?
A: The standard is 300 DPI and 3mm bleed. The full specification is 300 DPI images, 3mm bleed, 3mm safe zone, CMYK colour and PDF/X-1a:2001 export. Images below 300 DPI print blurry, insufficient bleed leaves a white edge after trimming, and RGB colour shifts blue or purple. ZprintPro provides a free 30-second AI file spec check.
