6. Foil Stamping or Spot UV? How to Choose a Finish

ZprintPro's 5-finish usage share (source: Packaging Box Pricing 2026 FAQ): foil stamping 70% of premium brands' choice, spot UV 15% for logos, embossing 12% for texture, debossing 3%, lamination 100% for protection. Tight budget? Spot UV. Premium? Foil stamping.

Finish cost uplift (source: Kraft vs Corrugated Box Comparison blog): foil +30-50%, spot UV +15-25%, embossing +20-30%. Best value is one focal finish on the cover logo.

  1. Lamination (gloss / matte): base protection and touch
  2. Spot UV: highlights logos, adds depth
  3. Foil stamping: metallic luster, luxury choice
  4. Embossing / debossing: ink-free raised texture
  5. Screen-printed UV: thick ink, strong tactile feel

More finishes is not better: each process adds a pass and drying cycle, extending lead time 1-2 days and stacking cost. The professional approach is one focal finish on the cover logo plus lamination elsewhere. Disposable delivery boxes need only lamination plus simple UV; collectible perfume and jewelry boxes justify foil plus embossing plus magnetic structure.
