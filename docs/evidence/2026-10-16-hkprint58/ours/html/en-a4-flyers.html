Frequently Asked Questions+
What is the minimum order quantity?

100 flyers. We also accept sample orders of 10-50 flyers for design proofing (rush fee applies).

Can I get same-day printing?

Yes. For orders up to 500 flyers with file confirmation by 11am (GMT+8), production and same-day dispatch are available. US delivery via DHL Express arrives 2-4 business days after dispatch.

Do you offer double-sided printing?

Yes. Double-sided 4-color CMYK is standard. Front-to-back registration precision within ±0.5mm. Submit two files (front and back) or one combined file with layers.

Can I add a QR code or coupon design?

Yes. QR codes, discount coupons, and tear-off reply cards are all supported. We can also design these for you at no extra charge.

What paper weight should I choose?

157g gloss art is the industry standard for A4 flyers. For premium feel, choose 200-250g matte. For high-volume distribution (under $0.10 per flyer), 128g gloss is the budget option.

Do you offer eco-friendly options?

Yes. FSC-certified recycled paper and soy-based inks are available. Carbon footprint calculation and eco-certification labels can be added on request.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order for flyer printing?+
ZprintPro offers A5 flyer printing with a minimum order of 10 copies. Whether for leaflet distribution, grand opening flyers, or event promotions, we support small orders perfect for startups and community outreach. See our Flyer Printing Masterclass for details.
Should I choose A5 or A4 flyers for my campaign?+
A5 flyers are compact and ideal for street handouts and mailbox drops at lower cost. A4 flyers suit detailed product information like menus and course schedules. Foil flyers in either size add a luxurious feel. Contact us for flyer distribution strategy advice.
Is same-day flyer printing available?+
Yes. A5 flyer printing (10-1000 copies) is available with same-day production. Confirm artwork before 11 AM (HKT) for same-day printing at our Asia facility, then 2-4 business day DHL / FedEx delivery — ideal for urgent events and campaigns.
Which industries benefit most from foil-stamped flyers?+
Foil-stamped flyers are especially suited for luxury, beauty, fine dining, and financial services. Foil stamping instantly elevates brand perception, perfect for grand openings, VIP event invitations, and annual report distribution. Response rates can be 30% higher than standard flyers.
What are the best strategies for flyer distribution?+
Effective flyer distribution strategies include: 1) Target area selection (e.g., downtown business districts, residential neighborhoods); 2) Timing alignment (rush hours, weekend markets); 3) Design with clear CTAs (QR codes, limited-time offers). See our Flyer Printing Masterclass for details.
What file specs do you need for flyer printing?+
Please provide AI, PDF, or EPS files at 300dpi or higher with 3mm bleed. Use CMYK color mode for full-color flyers and convert fonts to outlines. For double-sided flyers, supply separate files for front and back.
What folded leaflet styles do you offer?+
ZprintPro offers various folded leaflets: half-fold (2 panels), tri-fold (Z-fold/gate-fold), and four-fold map styles. Suitable for product catalogs, event programs, and menu design. Different folds affect reading flow; choose based on content hierarchy.
Do you deliver flyer orders internationally?+
Yes. ZprintPro delivers flyer orders worldwide from our Asia facility. US orders over $99 qualify for free shipping via DHL / FedEx (2-4 business days). For bulk leaflet distribution orders, delivery can be arranged directly to your distribution team.
Where is your factory? Do you print in-house?+
Every flyer order is printed, finished, and QC-checked at our own factory at No.1 Jiacheng Road, Pinghu Street, Longgang, Shenzhen — never brokered out. Retention samples are archived under ZPQC-2610-FL, traceable to press and shift.
