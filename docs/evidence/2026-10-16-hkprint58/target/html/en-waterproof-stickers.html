FAQ

Why do transparent stickers need white ink?

The transparent material itself does not have a white bottom layer. The white ink can hold up the color pattern and prevent the color from fading after light transmission.

Can the sticker be waterproof and placed in the refrigerator?

Synthetic paper, PVC or PET with appropriate coating can be waterproof and cold-resistant; for food refrigeration purposes, please specify the environment when placing an order.

Can special-shaped stickers be cut according to the pattern?

Can. Please provide an independent die line and reserve bleed and safety margins. We will check the file before production.

Will removable stickers leave glue after being removed?

Removable glue is suitable for short-term display. It is usually not easy to retain glue during normal use; the actual effect will still be affected by the surface material, temperature and usage time.

What is the minimum quantity for a general printing order?

Different products have different minimum printing volumes. Digital printing can generally be ordered from 10 to 100 pieces; for large orders, offset printing will be used to calculate a more favorable unit price.

Can I see the electronic draft or physical proof first?

Can. Electronic proofing can be arranged after the quotation is confirmed; for orders with higher requirements on color, paper material or structure, physical proofing can be arranged separately.

What format is required for printing archives?

It is recommended to provide PDF, AI or PSD, image resolution 300dpi, use CMYK color mode and reserve 3mm bleed.

Can delivery be made to all districts in Hong Kong?

Can be sent to Hong Kong Island, Kowloon, New Territories and outlying islands. For orders over a specified amount, you can enjoy free shipping arrangements with SF Express or designated areas.
