A5 vs A6 Effectiveness

A5 (148×210mm) is Hong Kong's standard flyer size—comfortable to hold and read, ideal for counter displays, direct mail, and event admission kits. A6 (105×148mm) costs approximately 55% of A5 and is perfect for street distribution and takeaway inserts. A4 folded leaflets triple content capacity while maintaining portability, making them ideal for complex services like education courses and financial products.

Special Sizes and Die-cut Flyers

Beyond standard sizes, square flyers (148×148mm), slim flyers (210×99mm), and rounded-corner flyers create visual differentiation. Die-cut flyers increase costs by 20-30% but significantly boost brand recall at trade shows and premium events.

Foil Flyer Finishing

Hong Kong Printers Association technical guidelines recommend keeping foil areas under 15% of flyer surface. Exceeding 20% creates visual overload that reduces perceived brand quality. 200gsm+ stock with rose gold or champagne foil achieves 92% client satisfaction.

— HKPA Technical Guidelines

Foil stamping transforms flyers from "advertising paper" to "collectibles." In the sea of homogenized flyers, foil is a dimensional strike that elevates ordinary paper to jewelry-box quality. Foil areas typically stay under 15% of flyer surface to accent rather than overwhelm. 200gsm+ stock with rose gold or champagne foil creates a warm, luxurious atmosphere perfect for weddings, luxury brands, and fine dining.

Flyer Design Psychology Principles

City University of Hong Kong marketing research shows high-contrast color schemes improve flyer reading rates by 40%. Sans-serif headlines achieve 25% better long-distance recognition than serif fonts. Consumers decide to keep or discard flyers within an average of 3 seconds.

— CityU Marketing Research 2024

Successful flyer design is not merely an aesthetic issue—it is applied psychology. Research shows consumers decide whether to keep or discard a flyer within 3 seconds of receiving it. Therefore, the "golden triangle"—approximately 60% of the area from upper-left to lower-right—must contain the most critical information: brand name, core selling point, and call-to-action (CTA). For color, high-contrast palettes increase readership by 40%; for typography, sans-serif headlines like Source Han Sans improve long-distance recognition by 25%. Flyer Paper and Finishing Options

ZprintPro material cost data shows 157gsm art paper is the standard choice for Hong Kong flyer printing, accounting for 71% of orders. 200gsm+ stock represents 18%, primarily from weddings, luxury brands, and fine dining.

— ZprintPro 2024 Material Report

157gsm art paper is the standard choice for Hong Kong flyer printing, balancing cost and print quality. For brands requiring premium texture, 200gsm+ stock communicates professionalism and trustworthiness. Matte lamination reduces light reflection for outdoor or bright environments; glossy lamination delivers more vibrant, saturated colors ideal for F&B and entertainment brands. Spot UV coating adds dimensional gloss to logos or key graphics, making flyers stand out from stacks. For high-end event invitations, foil stamping combined with heavy stock is a proven luxury combination.

Digital vs Offset Printing

Digital printing requires no plates, making it ideal for small runs (10-500 pieces) and urgent orders with 24-hour turnaround from file confirmation. Traditional offset requires plate-making but becomes cost-effective for large runs (1,000+), with per-unit costs dropping significantly as volume increases. For promotions requiring frequent design changes, digital printing's flexibility is irreplaceable; for stable long-term brand campaigns, offset's unit cost advantage becomes more pronounced.

Common Flyer Design Mistakes to Avoid

Many brands make three common mistakes in flyer design: information overload, attempting to cram all product details onto a single flyer so consumers cannot grasp the key point within 3 seconds; neglecting the call-to-action (CTA), leaving consumers unsure what to do after reading; and ignoring paper quality and finishing, using thin stock or low-quality printing that makes the brand appear cheap. Avoiding these mistakes requires focusing on one core message per flyer, making the CTA prominent and actionable, and selecting paper stock that aligns with brand positioning.

Hong Kong Flyer Distribution Regulations

When distributing flyers in Hong Kong, be aware of regulations under the Public Health and Municipal Services Ordinance. Distributing flyers on private premises or government property without permission may result in prosecution. Additionally, flyer content must not contain false or misleading statements, which could violate the Trade Descriptions Ordinance. Brands should seek legal advice before distribution to ensure compliance and choose legitimate distribution locations and methods.
