Product Details

Handle paper bags use riveted or folded reinforcements for cotton or flat paper handles—stronger than thin twine options for supermarket volumes, apparel twin-packs, or event kits. Kraft or white card stocks with base inserts improve load. Terms align with category content: paper rope, cotton rope, flat paper handles, reinforced base. Mark punch centers and target load tests on artwork; consider lamination for rainy-season protection.
